Added 20 new custom icons for RMS (Records Management System):

- `add_evidence` - Initiate evidence request workflow
- `booking` - Booking/arrest records identifier
- `car_crash` - Vehicle collision/accident records
- `cash_transfer` - Fund transfer transactions
- `fingerprint_scan` - Biometric data transfer to fingerprint machines
- `flask_exclamation` - Hazardous materials alert
- `folder_multiple_global` - Global/shared entities menu
- `gang` - Gang organization records
- `gang_member` - Individual gang member records
- `invoice_alarm` - Alarm billing invoices
- `permit_alarm` - Alarm permit module
- `permit_gun` - Firearm/concealed carry permits
- `photo_lineup` - Witness photo identification lineups
- `pill_search` - Narcotics investigation records
- `regenerate_billing` - Recalculate/regenerate invoices
- `renumber` - Correct ticket/citation identifiers
- `script_scale_balance` - Warrant records
- `script_shield_star` - Protection/restraining orders
- `shield_hand_left` - Use of force reports
- `trash_can_recycle` - Evidence disposal workflow
